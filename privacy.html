Privacy & Terms

Last updated November 26, 2026

Privacy

We don't collect any of your data. Zero Left Budget has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no tracking.

  • Your budget stays on your device. Everything you enter is saved in your browser's storage on this device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Imports stay on your device too. When you import EveryDollar CSV files, they are read inside your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Backups and exports are files you control. They are saved wherever you choose. We never see them.
  • Hosting. The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when the app is loaded. We don't have access to those records. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Deleting your data. Remove the app from your Home Screen, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. Download a backup first if you want to keep anything.

If you email us feedback, we only use your email to reply to you.
