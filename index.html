Zero Left Budget

Bring your EveryDollar budget over in a minute

The same zero-based budget you know, without the subscription. No account, no bank login. Your data stays on your device.

On everydollar.com, open each month, scroll to the bottom and click Download as CSV. Your transactions are most likely under This month's budget (EveryDollar's names look swapped). If you pick the wrong one, we'll tell you. Then choose all the files here at once. You'll see a preview before anything is imported.

  • Feels familiar. Budget groups, Planned / Spent / Remaining, and "left to budget" down to zero.
  • No subscription, no account. No sign-up and no bank connection.
  • Your data is yours. Old months are never locked. Export everything anytime.
  • Works everywhere. iPhone, Android and computer, even offline.
  • No AI, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
